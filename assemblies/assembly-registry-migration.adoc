= Migrating from {registry} {registry-v2} to {registry} 3.x

[role="_abstract"]
You can migrate from {registry} {registry-v2} to {registry} 3.x by deploying a new 3.x instance, exporting and importing your persisted data, and updating your client applications.

{registry} 3.x includes breaking changes that require a new deployment, data migration, and client application updates. There is no in-place upgrade path from v2 to v3.

ifndef::service-registry-downstream[]
NOTE: For general versioning policy, upgrade guidance within the 3.x series, and rollback procedures, see the versioning and support policy documentation in Additional resources.
