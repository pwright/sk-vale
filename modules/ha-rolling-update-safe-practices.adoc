= Safe update practices

[role="_abstract"]
Follow these practices to update your {registry} deployment safely and minimize risk.

Test in non-production first:: Validate the new version in a test environment.
Monitor during rollout:: Watch metrics and logs during the update.
Maintain minimum replicas:: Keep at least two replicas to ensure availability during updates.
Review release notes:: Check for breaking changes or migration steps.

IMPORTANT: For patch releases (for example, 3.1.0 to 3.1.1), rolling updates typically complete without issues. For major or minor version updates, always review the migration guide.
